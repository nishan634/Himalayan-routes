When to Visit

Best Time to Visit Nepal

Nepal's climate varies dramatically by season and altitude. Choose the right window for your adventure.

Peak Season
Oct — Nov
Autumn
Crystal-clear skies, dry trails, and the best mountain views of the year. Ideal for trekking, wildlife safaris, and cultural festivals. Days are warm, nights cool at altitude.
Best Mountain ViewsDashain FestivalAll Treks OpenWildlife Safari
Good Season
Mar — May
Spring
Rhododendrons bloom in a riot of colour across the hills. Stable weather, comfortable temperatures, and great for trekking before the monsoon arrives in June.
RhododendronsWarm TrailsEverest ClimbsHoli Festival
Monsoon
Jun — Sep
Summer
Heavy rain in most regions but lush green landscapes and very few tourists. Upper Mustang and rain-shadow areas are perfect now. Lower costs and peaceful temples.
Upper MustangLush GreeneryLower PricesFew Crowds
Off Season
Dec — Feb
Winter
Cold but clear at lower elevations. High passes may be closed but Kathmandu, Lumbini, Chitwan, and Pokhara remain excellent for cultural tours.
Kathmandu ValleyChitwan SafariLumbiniCrisp Air
